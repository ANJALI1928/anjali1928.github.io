Publications & Research

StatisticsHigh-Dimensional Data

High-Dimensional Analysis of Tumor Proteomics Data

MSc thesis on high-dimensional, low-sample-size tumor proteomics data to identify clinically relevant biomarkers. Proteins were imputed for 42 tumor patients using var-norm KNN, restoring usability from ~40% missing data.

Sparse PCA and sparse K-means clustering extracted discriminative protein features from 8K+ variables, identifying 50 key biomarkers explaining 97% variance and 24 proteins achieving ~63% classification accuracy.
